# Roadmap

Open directions for the household waste-sorting proposal. Each item leaves this file when it ships; git keeps finished plans. Every item has an issue with its plan.

## Direction

A clear, printable guide for every Thai family to sort household waste the German way, adapted to Thai collection, salengs, and climate. It started as a presentation and is becoming a guide: in Thai, readable by the people who will actually sort, with figures a reader can check.

## Now

**Dated, sourced figures** (stated 2026-11-19, [#1](https://github.com/UNKNOWN-X0/Household-Waste-Management-System/issues/1)). Prices, fines, and drop-off options have no date or source. *Done when* every price, fine, and drop-off claim has a date and a source or an "estimate" label.

## Later

**A Thai version** (stated 2026-11-19, [#2](https://github.com/UNKNOWN-X0/Household-Waste-Management-System/issues/2)). The site grows from a presentation into a guide for every Thai family, so all of it should be available in Thai.

## Not planned

**Accounts or tracking.** The checklist stays in the browser.
